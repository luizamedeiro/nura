Your spots aren't stubborn. They're getting sun every day.

Heat and UV both wake up the cells that make pigment. So you fade a spot in winter, spend one weekend at the beach, and it's back.

Where most routines go wrong

  1. 1

    A strong serum, no daily sunscreen. The spot fades, then returns.

  2. 2

    Sunscreen in the morning, never again. Sweat takes it off by lunch.

  3. 3

    Too many actives at once. Irritated skin makes more pigment, not less.

What's in each bottle, in plain words

Hover over a step to see what's insideTap a step to see what's inside

  1. PHA (gluconolactone) · pH 5.5 · sulfate-free

    What it does
    Lifts dead skin gently, without the sting of stronger acids.
    Why it's here
    Smooth, clean skin lets the serum work where it should.

  2. Tranexamic acid + niacinamide

    What it does
    Tranexamic acid tells your skin to stop overproducing pigment. Niacinamide keeps that pigment from reaching the surface.
    Why it's here
    Two ways to fade the same spot, gentle enough for twice a day.

  3. Panthenol + ceramide complex

    What it does
    Ceramides patch up your skin barrier. Panthenol calms and holds water.
    Why it's here
    Irritated skin makes more pigment. Calm skin fades faster.

  4. PA++++ · iron oxides

    What it does
    Blocks UVA and UVB. Iron oxides add protection from visible light, which can darken spots too.
    Why it's here
    No sunscreen, no results. This one dries matte and lasts 80 minutes in sweat or water.

What's not in it

No fragrance · No sulfates · No drying alcohol · No essential oils

Before you ask

Will it sting on sensitive skin?

PHA and tranexamic acid are two of the gentlest actives for spots. 89% of our testers felt no stinging. If your skin reacts easily, patch test on your jaw for 2 days first.

Can I use it while pregnant or breastfeeding?

Ask your doctor first. We'd rather you check than guess. You can show them the .

Can I keep my other products?

Yes, but pause other acids, retinol and vitamin C for these 28 days. Too many actives at once irritate skin, and irritation darkens spots.

What if I forget a step?

Don't double up. Just pick it up at your next routine. The one step to never skip is 04 Shield.

What happens after 28 days?

Keep going. Spots that took years to show usually need 2 to 3 cycles to fade further. Your refills arrive right on time.
